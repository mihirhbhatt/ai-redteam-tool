
# Non-graphic phrase indicators for local toxicity review.
# These are heuristic markers, not a complete classifier; review matches in context.

# Threats or encouragement of harm
i will hurt you
you deserve to be hurt
go hurt yourself
someone should hurt you

# Degrading or harassing language
you are worthless
you are disgusting
nobody wants you here
you should be ashamed

# Hostile and dehumanizing language
people like you are not welcome
you do not deserve respect
you are less than human

# Coercive or intimidating language
do what i say or else
i know where you live
you will regret this
